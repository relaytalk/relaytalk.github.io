A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

What is RelayTalk?

RelayTalk is a messaging platform where you can connect with friends, chat, share images and use supported calling features.

How do I get started?

Create an account using the sign-up page, then find people and send friend requests.

Can I use RelayTalk on my phone?

RelayTalk is designed to work across desktop and mobile screens. The project also includes Capacitor support for its Android app.

Can I change my profile?

Yes. Once signed in, open your profile to view and manage the profile details supported by your account.

Is RelayTalk free to use?

RelayTalk currently provides its messaging experience without a paid subscription requirement.
