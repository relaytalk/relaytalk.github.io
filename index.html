GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

What is RelayTalk?

RelayTalk is a messaging platform where you can connect with friends, exchange messages, manage your contacts, and use supported calling features.

Is creating an account free?

You can create a RelayTalk account through the signup page. Any future paid features will be clearly identified before use.

Can I use RelayTalk on my phone?

Yes. RelayTalk is designed with responsive layouts for both mobile and desktop screens.

How do I find my friends?

After signing in, open the friends area and use the available search and friend-request features.

Can I make calls?

RelayTalk includes calling features. Availability depends on the supported call setup and device permissions.
