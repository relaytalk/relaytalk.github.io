What is RelayTalk?

RelayTalk is a communication platform where you can connect with friends, chat and use available calling features.

How do I create an account?

Select "Get started" or "Create your account" and follow the signup process.

How can I find my friends?

Once signed in, use the friend search feature to look up someone by their username and send a friend request.

Can I make voice or video calls?

RelayTalk includes calling features. Availability can depend on your device, browser permissions and connection.

Is RelayTalk available on mobile?

RelayTalk is designed to work across modern screen sizes. The experience may vary depending on your browser and device.

I already have an account. Where do I log in?

Select "Log in" at the top of this page or use the button below to access your account.
