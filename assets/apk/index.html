Fast, private messaging and calls for friends.

Meet RelayTalk — the simple, private way to talk with the people who matter. Send messages instantly. Share photos in a tap. Make crystal-clear voice and video calls, encrypted end to end. All without ads, tracking, or any of the noise you've gotten used to.

Every message you send is protected. Every call you make is encrypted. And every notification arrives just how you want it — with full details or completely hidden, your choice. No groups, no feeds, no strangers. Just the friends you actually know, one conversation at a time.

Free. Fast. Yours. Download once, and start talking in under a minute.
