RelayTalk Guide

Everything you can do across RelayTalk, organized by page.

Home

  • See friends who are online at a glance
  • Search for new people to add
  • Tap any friend to start chatting
  • Check notifications and missed calls from the top-right bell

Chats

  • Send text messages instantly
  • Share images and photos in the conversation
  • Start an audio or video call from the top of the chat
  • Messages deliver in real time — no refresh needed

Friends

  • Browse all your connected friends
  • See who's online right now
  • Tap the message button to open a chat
  • Tap the call button to start a call — works even if they're offline
  • Tap a friend's name or avatar to view their profile

Profile

  • Update your display photo from camera or gallery
  • Write a short bio (up to 50 characters)
  • Enable or reset push notifications
  • Choose whether notifications show details (sender, message, avatar)

Friend's Profile

  • View a friend's photo, name, and username
  • Read their bio
  • Message or call them from the action buttons
  • All data is loaded live from their profile

Others

  • Add RelayTalk to your home screen for a native app experience
  • On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen before enabling notifications
  • Log out any time from Profile → Logout
  • Missed calls and friend requests are grouped under Alerts
