Install as PWA

What is a PWA?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is an app that runs directly in your browser and installs onto your home screen — just like a native app. It opens full-screen, works offline-friendly, and stays updated automatically. No store, no APK, no setup.

Install on Android (Chrome)
  • 1Open relaytalk.vercel.app in Chrome.
  • 2Tap the three dots at the top right.
  • 3Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • 4Confirm. RelayTalk appears on your home screen.
Install on iPhone (Safari)
  • 1Open relaytalk.vercel.app in Safari.
  • 2Tap the Share icon at the bottom.
  • 3Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  • 4Tap Add. Done.
Tip: Notifications work best when installed as a PWA or APK. In a normal browser tab, notifications may be limited by the browser.
