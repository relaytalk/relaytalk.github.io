About RelayTalk

Everything you might want to know — what it is, what it does, how your messages and calls are protected, and how to use it.

Last updated · September 2026

Details

RelayTalk is a lightweight messaging and calling app built for people who just want to talk — without clutter, ads, or noise.

What RelayTalk is

RelayTalk is a real-time chat app that lets you find friends by username, send text and image messages, and place voice or video calls. It runs in the browser and can be installed on your phone like a native app.

Who it’s for

  • People who want a simple way to message close friends and family.
  • Anyone who finds mainstream chat apps too heavy or too noisy.
  • Users who want their text messages and calls kept private.

What you can do

  • Create a free account in seconds and pick a username.
  • Search for friends by username and send friend requests.
  • Chat one-to-one in real time with text and images.
  • Start a voice or video call at any time — online or offline.
  • Get push notifications for new messages and missed calls.
  • Control whether notification previews show sender and content.

What RelayTalk is not

  • It is not a group chat or a channel-based platform — it is one-to-one.
  • It is not an anonymous network. You connect with people you know.
  • Image messages are not end-to-end encrypted (see Claims & Rights).

Features

Everything RelayTalk actually does, in plain language.

Claims & Rights

Straight answers about how your data is treated. No marketing language.

Text messages are encrypted

Text messages you send through RelayTalk are encrypted so that only the sender and the receiver can read them. RelayTalk cannot read the contents of your text messages.

Image messages are not end-to-end encrypted

Image messages are stored on our servers and the app owner can view them. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Please do not send sensitive or private images through RelayTalk.

Calls are encrypted

Voice and video calls placed through RelayTalk are encrypted in transit, so third parties cannot listen in on your call.

Your rights

  • You can edit or delete any message you have sent.
  • You can change your profile photo, bio and notification privacy at any time.
  • You can log out of any device, and your account stays safe.
  • You can ask for your account and its data to be removed.

Fair use

RelayTalk is meant for personal conversations between people who know each other. Harassment, spam, and illegal activity are not welcome and may result in account removal.

Help

Short answers to the things people ask most.

How do I find and add a friend?

Open the home page, tap Find friends, and search by their username. Send a request — once they accept, they appear in your friends list.

How do I open a chat?

Tap any friend in your list to open a chat instantly. You can also open a chat from their profile page.

How do I edit or delete a message?

Long-press your own message. Tap the pencil icon to edit, or the trash icon to delete. Edited messages show a small (edited) label, and deleted messages appear as “This message was deleted” for both people.

How do I start a call?

Tap the phone icon in the top-right corner of a chat, or use the call button next to a friend in your list. You can call whether they are online or offline.

What does the green or grey dot next to a friend mean?

A green dot means they are online right now. Grey means offline — you can still message and call them.

Notifications aren’t working. What do I do?

Open your Profile, then tap Reset under Notifications and enable them again. This creates a fresh subscription. Make sure notifications are allowed for RelayTalk in your device settings.

Can I hide message previews from my lock screen?

Yes. In your Profile, turn off Details Visible. Notifications will still arrive, but they won’t show the sender name, avatar, message content or images.

How do I install RelayTalk on my phone?

On iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: open it in Chrome and choose Install app from the menu.

Is RelayTalk free?

Yes. Creating an account and using messaging, calls and notifications is free.

I still need help. Where do I go?

Open the app and go to Profile → RelayTalk Guide. It explains every screen, page by page.
